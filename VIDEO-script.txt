QUINHAO - DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT (English)
Target: ~95 seconds. Read at a calm pace. Say the numbers slowly.
Stage directions are in [brackets] - do not read them aloud.


[SCREEN: top of the page, before scrolling]

Hi, I'm Elton Laice, from Mozambique. This is Quinhao - my entry for the
Transparency and Accountability track.

Quinhao is a Portuguese word. It means the share that is owed to someone by
right.

Mozambican law sends two point seven five percent of the mining and gas
production tax to the communities where the extraction happens. This tool shows
where that money actually went - and what nobody can explain.


[SCREEN: scroll to the comparison chart]

In 2024, those communities received three hundred and eighteen million meticais.
The provincial offices received eight hundred and forty.


[SCREEN: the EITI quote]

And this is not my accusation. This is EITI Mozambique, the government's own
transparency body, saying it cannot determine what criterion split that money
between communities.


[SCREEN: click "Dollars"]

In dollars, that's five million for every mining community in the country.


[SCREEN: map - switch to "Districts" - click Moatize]

It's also concentrated. Tete gets ninety-eight million. Eighty-seven of it lands
in one district - Moatize.


[SCREEN: search for "Benga" and select it]

And here is who it's for. Benga, a coal community in Moatize. Six years of
transfers. Forty point seven million meticais booked in their name.


[SCREEN: scroll to the bottom of the locality card]

Underneath: who is answerable, what the law allows the money to be spent on, and
three questions a resident has the right to ask.

Every figure is parsed from the official reports by a script. If a number doesn't
match the published total, the build fails.

Nobody in Benga was told. Now they can check. Thank you.

BEFORE YOU RECORD

1. Clean window:
   open -na "Google Chrome" --args --new-window --incognito https://eltonlaice.github.io/quinhao/
2. Do Not Disturb ON.
3. Set the page language to English.
4. Cmd+Shift+5 - "Record Selected Portion" - drag around the Chrome window.
5. OPEN "Options" AND SELECT YOUR MICROPHONE. Without this there is no audio.
6. Record. Stop with Cmd+Ctrl+Esc.

Output is a .mov on your Desktop. The form accepts mov, up to 250MB.

THREE RULES
- One take. If you stumble on a word, keep going.
- Say the numbers slowly. They are what people remember.
- If the audio fails, submit it silent anyway. A silent video counts. No video
  does not.
